Eastern Suburbs Memorial Park & Crematorium

A working cemetery and crematorium, not an ordinary leisure park. The appropriate visit begins with a purpose—finding a memorial, attending a service or a quiet, respectful look at the setting.

Use the operator’s location

Metropolitan Memorial Parks lists Eastern Suburbs Memorial Park and Crematorium at 12 Military Road, Matraville NSW 2036. Its location page offers a site map, funeral schedule and information on burial, ash memorials, cremation and chapels. Choose the information relevant to your visit rather than assuming one entrance will take you to every destination.[1]

Allow the site’s main purpose to lead

The operator describes the park as serving families since 1893 and as a place of reflection and remembrance. Keep noise low, allow services and grieving visitors privacy, and avoid photographing identifiable mourners or private ceremonies. These visitor courtesies are editorial advice; they are not a substitute for the operator’s current rules.[1]

Distinguish the building from the whole park

The licensed image is identified as the Eastern Suburbs Crematorium within the memorial park. It is a photograph of one building and its immediate setting, not a map of burial areas or a guarantee about chapel availability. The operator describes South and West chapels separately; confirm the correct destination if attending a service.[1]

Check a map and current visiting arrangements

Use the operator’s map before arrival and verify current visiting hours, which its page distinguishes from office hours. If searching for a particular person’s memorial, establish that location before wandering between unrelated areas. Check photography permissions for any organised shoot and confirm mobility arrangements directly. Do not infer unrestricted recreational use from the word park.[1]
