St Agnes’ War Memorial Church

An active place of worship with a precise parish-published identity. An architectural look and participation in worship are different visits; make space for the people using the church.

Find the correct church

The Parish of the Holy Martyrs lists St Agnes’ War Memorial Church at 509 Bunnerong Road, Matraville. Its homepage also lists St Andrew’s Church at Malabar and The Reconciliation Church at Phillip Bay. Use the full name and address when arranging a visit so that these three separate churches are not confused.[1]

Read the exterior without inventing a history

The retained licensed photograph is specifically identified as St Agnes Catholic Church, Matraville. It records the church façade, cross and approach. This guide does not assign an architect, construction date or heritage status from appearance alone. The War Memorial wording comes from the current parish’s own published church name.[1]

A church is not a drop-in attraction

The parish describes itself as a community of believers, with worship, sacraments, ministries and service to others at the centre of its mission. Plan accordingly: do not interrupt a service for photographs, assume an interior is open all day or infer public event access from an exterior image. Ask the parish about the purpose and timing of your proposed visit.[1]

Check the arrangements that matter to you

Use the parish’s current information for worship times and contact arrangements, rather than a timetable copied into a static local guide. If you need a step-free entrance or assistance, confirm the exact entrance and current arrangements directly. The photographed approach does not certify the accessibility of every route or interior.
