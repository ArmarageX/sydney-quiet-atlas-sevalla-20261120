Yarra Bay

A Botany Bay shore near La Perouse, distinct from Matraville and from the open ocean at Maroubra. The licensed photograph is identified as Yarra Bay at Phillip Bay; older file names elsewhere can loosely label the wider area Matraville.

Find the right bay

The photographer's file description identifies this scene as Yarra Bay at Phillip Bay. Yarra Bay Sailing Club also describes its position as on the beach at Yarra Bay, near La Perouse. Together these give a precise place identity without claiming that all of the southern peninsula is within Matraville.[1][2]

Shoreline and sailing are different plans

The club calls itself the Yarra Bay 16ft Skiff Sailing Club and describes licensed club facilities and dining. Its beachfront setting is a landmark, not an assurance that visitors can use sailing equipment or join a race without arrangements. If the purpose of your visit is sailing, contact the club about the activity rather than treating an ordinary beach outing as a booking.[2]

Do not infer today's conditions from this image

The photograph shows a sandy bay and water in historical conditions. It cannot establish current water quality, depth, weather, access or patrol coverage. Use current local advice and beach signs before deciding whether to enter the water. The guide deliberately makes no safe-swimming or always-calm claim.

Choose a useful stopping point

For a short outing, keep the plan centred on seeing this particular shore instead of adding an assumed continuous walk around every neighbouring bay. Confirm the route you intend to use and your return transport. Dining at a waterfront club is optional and separate: current service hours, visitor requirements and menus should be checked with that business rather than inferred from this guide.[2]
