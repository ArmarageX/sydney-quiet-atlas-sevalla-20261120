Congwong Beach

A sandy bay within the La Perouse area of Kamay Botany Bay National Park. This is a separate excursion south of Matraville, with access details worth checking before choosing it for a group.

Know the approach

NSW National Parks describes a 100-metre path from the southern end of Cann Park and explicitly notes steps. An alternative is Congwong trail from Henry Head Lane near the park gates. The official page states there is no wheelchair access; do not use a short distance as a substitute for an accessibility check.[1]

Choose beach time or a longer walk

The park page links Congwong with Henry Head and Cape Banks walks. That does not mean you need to combine them. A beach visit can stand on its own; if you choose a walking extension, read that track's distance, grade and alerts before leaving the beach. Allow separately for the return journey.[1]

Check water quality before swimming

NPWS asks visitors to check the latest Beachwatch forecast and advises avoiding swimming during and for 24 hours after heavy rain. Its general description of sheltered water is not a guarantee of safe conditions on your visit. Read current signs, weather information and water advice rather than relying on the appearance of the photograph.[1]

Leave room for a change of plan

Carry water, a hat and sunscreen, and check park alerts before travelling. Access to a beach does not imply that nearby historic attractions are open. In particular, do not assume a visit to Congwong includes access to Bare Island: check that attraction's separate closure notice and keep any mainland viewing within the permitted public area.[1]
