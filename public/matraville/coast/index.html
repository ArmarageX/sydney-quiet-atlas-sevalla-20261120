Beaches beyond Matraville

Four distinct stretches of coast for a considered outing from Matraville. These are nearby extensions in Malabar, Maroubra, Phillip Bay and La Perouse, not beaches inside Matraville. Choose a shore by its setting and access rather than treating every calm-looking bay as a swimming recommendation.

Malabar Beach

Malabar · nearby coastal extension

A Long Bay shoreline visit with a grassy park behind the sand. Malabar is a separate suburb east of Matraville; use this guide to plan a shore-based pause rather than assuming an unpatrolled beach is safe for a swim.

Maroubra Beach

Maroubra · nearby surf-coast extension

A different coastal character from the bays south of Matraville: an open-beach visit anchored by a real local surf lifesaving club. Maroubra is its own suburb, not another name for Matraville's centre.

Read the place notes →

Yarra Bay

Phillip Bay · nearby Botany Bay extension

A Botany Bay shore near La Perouse, distinct from Matraville and from the open ocean at Maroubra. The licensed photograph is identified as Yarra Bay at Phillip Bay; older file names elsewhere can loosely label the wider area Matraville.

Read the place notes →

Congwong Beach

La Perouse · nearby national-park extension

A sandy bay within the La Perouse area of Kamay Botany Bay National Park. This is a separate excursion south of Matraville, with access details worth checking before choosing it for a group.
