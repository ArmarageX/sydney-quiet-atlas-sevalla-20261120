Bob Clarke Memorial Reserve

A compact remembrance setting on Anzac Parade, rather than a long exercise route. Keep the character of this memorial distinct from the much larger sporting landscape at Heffron Park.

Identify the place accurately

The photographer’s file title and description identify Bob Clarke Memorial Reserve at Chifley, on Anzac Parade. The retained photograph shows a landscaped grassed area, trees, flagpoles and a memorial feature. Chifley is a nearby suburb; the reserve is not being presented as within Matraville.[1]

Choose a short, respectful stop

This is a better editorial fit for a brief pause and a careful look at a memorial than for an assumed all-day park itinerary. Give anyone reading or paying respects room, avoid using memorial structures as seating or play equipment, and leave commemorative objects undisturbed. These are suggested visitor courtesies, not a quoted council regulation.

Do not invent the memorial’s story

The photo establishes the named place and visible setting; it does not establish a biography, a dedication date or a full account of the person commemorated. Read any onsite inscription and seek council’s historical information before repeating a story. This guide deliberately avoids filling those gaps with an inferred history.[1]

Check the practical details separately

Council’s page was access-blocked during research. No toilets, opening hours, playground, accessible path or current ceremony schedule is certified here. Check your actual route and current public access before travelling, especially if a step-free entrance or a particular facility is essential. Use a different outing if those needs cannot be confirmed.
