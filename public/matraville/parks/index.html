Parks & headland walks

Choose between a substantial national-park walk, a headland with conditional access, a large sporting landscape and a compact memorial reserve. These are not interchangeable green spaces. Matraville is the starting locality; Malabar, La Perouse and Chifley are labelled nearby extensions.

Malabar Headland National Park

Malabar / Maroubra · nearby headland extension

A coastal landscape between two beaches, with an important distinction between its western and eastern sections. Plan around access restrictions rather than assuming every marked path is available.

Read the place notes →

Henry Head walking track

La Perouse · nearby national-park extension

A defined return walk to a coastal headland, rather than another beach stop. This route needs its own time allowance and a decision about whether to return or continue farther.

Read the place notes →

Heffron Park

Matraville / Maroubra locality · park-specific outing

A broad open sporting landscape, quite unlike a sheltered beach or a bushwalking track. Choose a specific purpose and meeting point instead of using the park’s name as if it were one small entrance.

Read the place notes →

Bob Clarke Memorial Reserve

Chifley · nearby memorial-reserve extension

A compact remembrance setting on Anzac Parade, rather than a long exercise route. Keep the character of this memorial distinct from the much larger sporting landscape at Heffron Park.
