Henry Head walking track

A defined return walk to a coastal headland, rather than another beach stop. This route needs its own time allowance and a decision about whether to return or continue farther.

Start with the official distance

NSW National Parks lists Henry Head walking track as 4km return, Grade 3, with a suggested 45 minutes to 1 hour 15 minutes. Treat that as the published guide, not a personalised completion time. Allow more time for rests, photographs and your own pace, and check alerts before starting.[1]

Agree on a starting point

The official page gives two options: La Perouse Museum at Cann Park on Anzac Parade, or the carpark near the park entry gates on Anzac Parade opposite Goorawahl Avenue. Agree on one before meeting others. The same source describes the walk passing coastal scenery towards Endeavour Lighthouse at Henry Head.[1]

Keep the optional extension optional

At Henry Head, NPWS describes either retracing your steps or continuing a further 1.2km to Cape Banks. That extension is separate from the stated return route. Decide before proceeding whether your available daylight, water, energy and transport plan support a longer walk; do not accidentally turn a modest outing into a different itinerary.[1]

Look without making a wildlife promise

The park page describes birdwatching and historic World War II battlements along the route. These are reasons to look carefully, not invitations to enter closed structures or approach wildlife. Wear suitable footwear, keep away from exposed edges and consult current access information. A historical coastline photograph cannot establish today’s track surface.[1]
